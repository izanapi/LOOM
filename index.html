Settings

Pluck. Tap a vertical string, or brush across several. Faster strokes play louder. The strings rise in pitch from left to right.

Weave. Hold for a moment to couple a vertical string to the nearest horizontal string. Keep holding and move to another crossing. The old resonance fades into the new one. Pull a little sideways for more intensity. Use several fingers to weave independent voices; sharing a horizontal string strengthens its resonance.

The six threads. HARM opens upper partials. BLOOM grows a harmony from your scale. FIFTH adds an open fifth and octave. DUST scatters small grains of a plucked string. ECHO passes neighboring notes back and forth. ROOT draws a low tonic beneath your note. FIFTH and HARM use exact fifths, which can add color beyond the selected scale.

Loop. Press REC, then play. Record two bars of plucks and crossings, including how long each crossing is held. Playback weaves them again alongside your fingers. PAUSE keeps the phrase; CLEAR erases it. Changing key or scale retunes the loop. Loops are kept until the page is reloaded.

FLOW. An optional quiet accompaniment follows your tuning and tempo. It is not recorded.

Space. ECHO is a stereo delay; HALL is a long room tail. These controls affect both plucks and held resonances.

Keys. A S D F G H J, then Q W E R T Y U play the 14 strings. Hold a key to couple it. Keys 1–6 choose the horizontal string for your next note. Space controls the loop. Esc stops the sound.
