BRUSH THE CORNER · HOLD TO WEAVE

SWEEP THROUGH THE STRINGS
2 BARS · CROSSINGS + NOTES

Settings

The corner. Vertical strings gather on the right; horizontal strings gather below. The upper-left stays quiet. Begin there and sweep into the strings, or circle through the lower-right corner.

Brush. Swipe through several strings to catch a little cascade of notes. In the lower-right, each stroke also wakes short horizontal resonances. A quick diagonal sweep mixes several threads; slower strokes leave more space.

Catch a phrase. The lower strings can be played on their own. Harmony threads, except DRONE, follow the last vertical string you played, even after you lift that finger. Hold a lower thread and pluck a new vertical string to retune it. Texture brushes can also catch recent notes. Brush down their left ends to pass a phrase through several colors.

Depth. The upper vertical string plays its note. Below the middle, a fifth joins it; near the bottom, an octave joins too. The string changes color at the marked +5th and +8ve boundaries. Slide down the same string to hear it open.

Weave. Hold a crossing for a moment to grow a sustained resonance. Keep holding and move to another crossing. The old resonance fades into the new one. Pull a little sideways for more intensity. Several fingers can weave independently.

Twelve threads. BLOOM / HARM / DUST / FIFTH / SILK / THIRD / SHIMMER / OCTAVE / ECHO / ROOT / MIRAGE / DRONE. Soft swells, grains, bowed tones, tremolo and reversed grains alternate with partials, harmony, octaves and an independent deep DRONE. Harmony threads use the selected VOICE. Texture releases linger for about 3.5 seconds; DRONE sustains at a steady level after release; CLEAR stops it. Its thicker bottom string sounds one low KEY root everywhere, including in ARP mode. Sliding and vertical notes never retune it. Brush down the left ends to travel through all twelve.

PLUCK / CHORD / ARP. Play one string, open a three-note scale chord, or hold to cycle through its notes on eighth-note beats. In ARP, horizontal strings cycle through their own resonance notes, including when held on their left ends. All three modes weave and record into the loop.

Tunings. Hijaz is the starting point. Rast and Bayati use quarter-tone approximations; ↓50 on a note means 50 cents below the named pitch. These are fixed, maqam-inspired tunings for exploration, not a reproduction of the full melodic tradition. Qanun, Santur and Oud are synthesized string colors.

Loop. Press REC, then play two bars. Plucks, brushed resonances and held crossings are recorded together. Playback weaves them again alongside your fingers. PAUSE keeps the phrase; CLEAR erases it. Key and scale retune the loop. Reloading the page clears the recording.

FLOW / Space. FLOW adds quiet accompaniment and is not recorded. ECHO is stereo delay; HALL adds a long room tail. Both affect plucks and resonances.

Keys. A S D F G H J, then Q W E R T Y U play the 14 strings. Hold a key to couple it; keys 1–0, − and = choose the horizontal string for the next note. Space controls the loop. Esc stops the sound.
